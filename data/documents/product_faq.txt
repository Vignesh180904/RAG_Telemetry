Product FAQ - CloudSync Pro

What is CloudSync Pro?
CloudSync Pro is a file synchronization and backup application that automatically syncs your files across all your devices and keeps encrypted backups in the cloud.

Pricing Plans
CloudSync Pro offers three plans: the Basic plan at $5 per month with 100GB storage, the Pro plan at $12 per month with 1TB storage, and the Business plan at $25 per user per month with unlimited storage and team collaboration features.

Supported Platforms
CloudSync Pro is available on Windows, macOS, Linux, iOS, and Android. Files sync automatically across all installed devices within a few seconds of a change.

Data Encryption
All files are encrypted using AES-256 encryption both during transfer and while stored on our servers. Only the account owner holds the decryption keys, following a zero-knowledge encryption model.

Offline Access
Files marked as "available offline" are stored locally and remain accessible without an internet connection. Changes made offline sync automatically once the device reconnects.

File Version History
CloudSync Pro keeps up to 30 days of version history for every file on the Pro and Business plans, and 7 days on the Basic plan. Users can restore any previous version from the version history panel.

Sharing and Collaboration
Users can share files and folders via a secure link with optional password protection and expiration dates. Business plan users can set granular permissions, including view-only, comment, and edit access.

Storage Limit Alerts
Users receive an email notification when they reach 90% of their storage limit, with the option to upgrade their plan directly from the notification.

Customer Support
Support is available via email for all plans, with live chat support included in the Pro and Business plans. Business plan customers additionally get a dedicated account manager and priority phone support.

Account Cancellation
Users can cancel their subscription at any time from the account settings page. Upon cancellation, files remain accessible in read-only mode for 30 days before being permanently deleted.

System Requirements
CloudSync Pro requires at least 4GB of RAM and 500MB of free disk space for the desktop client. An active internet connection is required for initial setup and ongoing synchronization.

Two-Factor Authentication
CloudSync Pro supports two-factor authentication using an authenticator app or SMS codes. Enabling two-factor authentication is strongly recommended and can be turned on from the security settings page.
